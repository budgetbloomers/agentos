Attendance % and SPD are always recalculated from the stored inputs — they can never drift from the manual numbers. Click Edit on any row to reopen that date in the Input tab and update it.

DoD columns compare each day against the previous tracked day (weekends/gaps are skipped). Attendance moves in percentage points; Sales (Afrilead + JAG + VT combined), SPD and Avg GWP move in %. "—" means there is no earlier day to compare against, or the previous day was zero.
