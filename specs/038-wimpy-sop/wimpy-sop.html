For every ChatterBox colleague

★What this means for ChatterBox staff

Wimpy Order is a staff benefit. It gives every colleague on the Agent List a hot breakfast or lunch from the Wimpy kitchen, ordered from your phone or desk in under a minute, at staff prices, on account — you do not pay at the counter. This page is the plain-language version of the deal; the rest of the document is how it works.

You buy on credit

No cash, no card, no transfer when you order or collect. Each order goes on your ChatterBox staff account under your CB number, and the total printed on your slip is the amount you owe for that order.

You pay staff prices

Every priced item is below the Wimpy list price — about 20% off across the menu and a third off the toasteds and the Dagwood. The saving is shown on each card, and the price is fixed in the app, so the slip is never a surprise.

Your account is yours alone

Only you can order on your account: your CB number and your Wimpy password open it, every order is recorded against your name, and only you can see your history. That is also why the password must be your own, not the start password.

Your record is always available

My orders lists everything you have bought since 7 October 2026, with the date, the items and the total — your own statement, any time, inside or outside the ordering window.

1Purpose and scope

Wimpy Order is how The ChatterBox floor orders breakfast and lunch from the on-site Wimpy kitchen on weekday mornings. This procedure exists so that every person who orders, cooks, supervises or maintains the service does the same thing the same way, and so that a new team leader can run it from this document alone.

In scope

  • Using the app: signing in, the ordering window, the menu, placing an order, order history, the Wimpy password.
  • Running the app: onboarding and leavers, password resets, the kitchen's board, daily and weekly checks, errors and escalation.
  • Owning the app: what data it keeps and where, how prices, menu and hours are changed, released and verified, and how this document is kept true.

Out of scope

  • The kitchen's own cooking, stock and cash procedures.
  • Agent Analytics, VAS Analytics and the other AgentOS apps, except where they touch Wimpy (accounts come from the Agent List; a team-leader password reset affects Wimpy).
  • Payment. Wimpy Order places orders; settlement with the kitchen is handled outside the app.
Principle. The server decides. Every rule in this document — hours, prices, quantities, who may see which history — is enforced by the service, not by the page. A page that looks open after 09:00 still cannot order; a changed price on screen is never charged.

The life of an order

1

Sign in with a CB number (managers: username) and the Wimpy password. The session lasts 12 hours and opens nothing but Wimpy.

2

Inside the window (07:00–09:00, Monday to Friday, South African time) the person adds items from today's menu, sets quantities, and fills in CB number, name, surname, phone and optional notes.

3

The service re-prices the order from its own menu, checks the window, the identity and every line, and only then sends the slip to the kitchen system. If the kitchen does not answer, the order stays on the confirm screen and the person sends it again.

4

The kitchen said yes: the service records the order in the ledger and, in the background, writes the person's contact and the order's card to the Wimpy Pipeline at New Order. The page shows the total, the slip and the time.

5

The kitchen works the board: New Order → Order Processing → Ready for Collection → Completed. The person sees the stage in My orders whenever they look.

6

If the board write failed (HighLevel down, a refused card), the service retries with growing gaps, and My orders heals the person's own late cards each time they open it. After eight failed tries a row is marked stuck and management is alerted.

5Access, sign-in and passwords

5.1 Who has access

  • Every active account on the Agent List. When the Agent List upload creates an account, a database trigger grants it the Wimpy app in the same transaction. Leavers lose the account, and with it Wimpy, when the list drops them.
  • Managers and management staff with an AgentOS account order too. Everyone orders; there is no "staff only" menu.
  • Nobody is added to Wimpy by hand. An active agent without the app shows as a warning row in the Agent List integrity report; the fix is the Agent List, not the Wimpy service.

5.2 Signing in

1

Open https://phoenix-wimpy.vercel.app. The sign-in screen says "Chatterbox × Wimpy · Weekday Value Menu" and shows the hours in its footer.

2

Type your CB number in any spelling (CB145, CB-145, 145). Managers without a CB number type their username ("Managers: type your username here.").

3

Type your Wimpy password. On a new account that is the start password wimpy123. Your AgentOS password does not open Wimpy.

4

You land on the menu, signed in under your CB number (shown at the top). The session lasts 12 hours or until you sign out.

What you typedWhat the app saysWhy

A wrong password, an unknown CB number, or a disabled accountThat CB number and password do not matchOne plain message; the app never says which half was wrong
Five wrong passwords on one account within 15 minutesAccount locked after repeated failures. Try again in a few minutes.The lock lasts 15 minutes and is shared with the AgentOS sign-in: Wimpy guesses lock both doors

5.3 The Wimpy password rules

  • Start password: wimpy123 for every account until the person sets their own. While still on it, the menu shows a nudge: "You are still on the Wimpy start password. Choose your own so nobody else can order in your name."
  • Your own password: at least 10 characters; not the same as the current one; not wimpy123. Set inside the app (section 11). Changing it signs out every other Wimpy session of your account.
  • Forgotten: ask your team leader to reset your AgentOS password. That reset also clears your Wimpy password, so Wimpy answers to wimpy123 again. Set a new one straight away.
  • Separate from AgentOS: the two passwords never sync. Changing one never changes the other.
Never share a Wimpy password or order under someone else's CB number. The order form checks that the CB number typed is the signed-in person's own; an order for a colleague is refused. Every order is recorded against the account that placed it.

Inside the window, signed in.

1

Choose. Tap Add to order on an item. One tap adds one. Use + and − in the cart ("🛒 Your order") to set the quantity, from 1 to 10 per line. Add as many different items as you like.

2

3

Next: your details. On "Who is this order for?" type your CB number (it must be your own; managers type their username), your name, your surname (letters only, 2 to 40 characters), your phone number (a South African number, e.g. 082 123 4567) and, if needed, Notes for the kitchen (optional, up to 200 characters on the page). 9What the kitchen receives

The moment the service accepts an order it sends one slip to the kitchen system. The slip is plain text so it prints and forwards cleanly. Prices on the slip are the service's, never the page's.

The slip

Name: Thandi Dlamini
CB Number: CB287
Phone: 0821234567
On the Agent List as: Thandiwe Dlamini

---

2 × CHEESE BURGER — R147.80

---

1 × BIG CHAMPION (TODAY’S BIG ONE) — R115.90

---

TOTAL: R263.70

Illustrative names. "On the Agent List as" appears only when the typed name differs from the Agent List's. Notes are free text (up to 200 characters on the page) and are not a customisation the service enforces — the kitchen reads them as a request. The phone number is printed in the ten-digit form whatever was typed.

Guarantees

  • One slip per accepted order. A refused order (window, identity, pricing) never reaches the kitchen.
  • The kitchen's answer decides. Only a "yes" from the kitchen system makes the order real. A "no" or silence leaves the order on the person's page with Retry.
  • Prices are the service's. If the page's total disagrees with the service's, the service's total is used and the disagreement is recorded as price_mismatch.
  • The slip and the card agree. The ledger row and the board card are written from the same accepted order: same items, same quantities, same total.
  • Timeout: 15 seconds. After that the person is told the kitchen did not answer and can send the same order again from the confirm screen.

Then, in the background

  • The order is written to the ledger (wimpy_orders) under the account that placed it.
  • The person's contact (once) and the order's card are written to the Wimpy Pipeline at New Order. A failure here never fails the order; it is retried (section 12.3).

12Kitchen procedure and the order board

12.1 Receiving

  • Slips arrive in the kitchen system from 07:00; the last one can arrive at 08:59:59. Prepare from the slip's lines and quantities. Notes are requests, not instructions the app verified.
  • A slip always carries name, CB number and phone. Call the number on the slip if something is unclear.
  • If no slips arrive during a weekday window, tell Operations immediately (section 16): either nobody ordered, or the hand-off is down, and the ledger tells which.

12.2 Working the board

In HighLevel (the Wimpy sub-account → Opportunities → Wimpy Pipeline) every accepted order appears as one card at New Order, named for the person and the order. Move it as the order progresses:

StageMove the card here when
New OrderWhere the service places it. Nothing to do.
Order ProcessingPreparation starts.
Ready for CollectionIt is on the counter. The person sees this in My orders.
CompletedCollected. Close every card by the end of the morning.
  • One person may have several cards in a morning — one per order. The sub-account setting "Allow duplicate opportunities" must stay ON (agency-level); without it a person's second order gets no card.
  • Do not delete cards — a deleted card shows the person "No longer on the kitchen board" in My orders. Completed is the end state.

12.3 Cards that are late or missing

  • The card is written in the background after the kitchen says yes. If HighLevel is unavailable the write is retried after 2, 5, 10, 20, 40, 80 and 160 minutes; the eighth failure marks the row stuck and raises the wimpy-hl-stuck alert for management.
  • On Vercel's current plan the scheduled retry runs once a day at 05:07 SAST; between runs, a person's own late cards are repaired whenever they open My orders.
  • A stuck row means the order was delivered to the kitchen and only its card is missing. Management re-queues stuck rows after fixing the cause (section 14).

13Team-leader procedures

13.1 A new agent

1

The agent must be on the Agent List. The list upload creates the account and grants Wimpy; nothing else is needed.

2

Tell them the address https://phoenix-wimpy.vercel.app, that they sign in with their CB number and the start password wimpy123, and the hours 07:00–09:00, Monday to Friday. The sign-in screen does not print the start password; you do.

3

Ask them to change the password on their first sign-in (section 11). Check within the week that the start-password nudge has gone.

13.2 "I can't sign in"

  1. Is it the Wimpy password they are typing, not the AgentOS one? If they never changed it, it is wimpy123.
  2. Is the account locked ("Account locked after repeated failures")? Wait 15 minutes. The same lock also blocks Agent Analytics.
  3. "Too many wrong sign-ins from this network"? The office connection hit the flood guard; wait ten minutes and warn the floor to stop guessing.
  4. Still no? Reset per 13.3.

13.3 Forgotten Wimpy password — the reset

1

Reset the person's AgentOS password the normal way (the team-leader reset in Agent Analytics / VAS, which returns it to the AgentOS start password).

2

That reset also clears the Wimpy password: Wimpy now answers to wimpy123 again. Tell the person.

3

They sign in to Wimpy with wimpy123 and set a new password straight away (section 11), and set a new AgentOS password in that app.

13.4 Leavers and movers

  • A leaver dropped from the Agent List loses the account and Wimpy with it. Nothing to do in Wimpy.
  • A mover between campaigns keeps the same CB number and account; Wimpy is unaffected.

13.5 What a team leader does not do

  • Order on behalf of an agent, or hand out a Wimpy password that is not the start password.
  • Change menu, prices or hours. Those are Operations decisions released by engineering (section 18).

17.2 Rules

  • Own history only. The history route ignores every parameter sent to it and answers from the session's owner alone (section 10).
  • A Wimpy session opens Wimpy only. It is refused by every other AgentOS route, and the AgentOS Worker answers 410 Gone on every old Wimpy route.
  • Secrets live on the host. The database key, the HighLevel key and location and the cron secret are environment settings on Vercel (or the container's .env), never in the code, the image, a reply or a log. Errors are redacted before they are stored.
  • Minimum data on the slip. Name, CB, phone, lines, total, notes — nothing else travels to the kitchen.
  • Retention is not yet defined. Proposed: ledger rows older than 12 months are archived or deleted by engineering on the owner's instruction; HighLevel cards follow the sub-account's own retention. The owner decides and this section is revised.

17.3 POPIA note

The app processes employee personal information (name, CB number, phone) for one purpose: fulfilling a lunch order the person placed. It is not used for anything else and is not shared beyond the kitchen and the order board. Requests to view or delete a person's Wimpy data go to the Operations owner.

The release procedure

1

Approve. The Operations owner approves the change in writing (what, why, from when). Prices come from the brief; "do not randomly change these prices" stands.

2

Change and test. Engineering edits the service and runs npm test in wimpy-service/ (menu, sign-in, history, page sweep, hosting suites) plus the drift and removal guards. All green or no release.

3

Release. Deploy to Vercel from a copy outside the git repository (the hosting account blocks deployments attributed to a non-member author). Secrets are never re-typed; they already sit on the project.

4

Verify live. Run the live check (verify-wimpy-live): a throwaway sign-in, the menu, one order marked TEST to the kitchen, My orders, the retry, sign-out. Tell the kitchen a TEST slip is coming.

5

Record. Commit; update this SOP's affected sections and its revision table; tell team leaders what changed and from when.

Emergency changes

If orders are reaching the kitchen wrong (Sev 1), engineering may roll back to the previous Vercel deployment first and seek approval after. Every emergency change is still recorded under step 5 the same day.

20Technical reference

Hosting

  • Vercel project phoenix-wimpy, account budgetbloomers; production address https://phoenix-wimpy.vercel.app.
  • Vercel runs src/app.mjs as the root Node entry point (no serverless split); the page and the API are one server.
  • A container image (node 24 alpine, no install step, health check) runs the same code with a 20-minute in-process retry timer.
  • Settings (five, all sensitive): database URL and key, HighLevel key and location id, cron secret. Set with scripts/env.mjs --vercel from the git-ignored env files.
  • Retry schedule on Vercel: 7 3 * * * (05:07 SAST) with Authorization: Bearer CRON_SECRET.

The AgentOS side

  • The Worker answers 410 Gone on /api/wimpy/* and /api/portal/wimpy/*, naming the new address, and refuses a Wimpy session on every route.
  • The VAS site forwards /wimpy/* to the service (302). The Wimpy card was removed from the VAS Apps page on 9 Oct 2026.
  • Health reads kv:wimpy-hl-status and kv:wimpy-ledger-failed, which the service writes (counts only, no person).

22Wimpy Order — the one-page card

Where

phoenix-wimpy.vercel.app

Works on your phone and the floor PCs.

When

07:00 – 09:00

Monday to Friday, South African time. Outside that you can read the menu and your orders; you cannot order.

Sign in

Your CB number (any spelling) and your Wimpy password. Managers: username.

New account? The start password is wimpy123. Change it on day one: 🔑 Change password, 10+ characters.

Five wrong tries locks you out for 15 minutes — on Wimpy and on Agent Analytics.

Order in five taps

  1. Add to order (tap once per item; + / − for quantity, up to 10)
  2. Next: your details
  3. Your own CB number, name, surname, phone; notes if you need them
  4. Review order
  5. Confirm & send to Wimpy

Today's Big One

Mon Mega Breakfast · Tue Dagwood · Wed Big Bacon & Cheese · Thu Big Champion · Fri Fully Loaded Dagwood. Only today's can be ordered.

Collect

Open 🧾 My orders. When your order says Ready for Collection, fetch it. "Not on the kitchen board yet" means the kitchen has it; the board is catching up.

If something goes wrong

  • Can't sign in → are you typing the Wimpy password? Never changed it? It is wimpy123. Still no → your team leader resets it (back to wimpy123).
  • "The order could not be sent … press Retry" → press Confirm & send to Wimpy again. Still failing → tell your team leader now, inside the window.
  • Page looks odd → Ctrl+Shift+R.

Rules

  • Order in your own name only. Every order is recorded against your account.
  • Items come as they are. Notes are a request to the kitchen, not a guarantee.
  • Prices are fixed in the app; what the slip says is what you pay.
