The process

1End-to-end flow

Two records are made of every order, by design. The service writes the order ledger (what the person sees in My orders), and the n8n automation writes the payroll sheet and the kitchen board. Payroll deducts only after the two agree (section 3).
Step 2

2The automation and the payroll sheet

Every accepted order is sent by the service to one n8n workflow. The workflow has four nodes; the last two are its two jobs: the kitchen board and the payroll sheet.

What the sheet holds

One row is appended the moment an order is accepted. The columns are the person's identity as they signed in and ordered, the total the service charged, and the lines in words:

Step 5

3Accounts, payments and the verified deduction

Nobody pays at the counter: every order is on the person's staff account and is recovered as a payroll deduction, once per pay period, after verification against the service's own ledger.

3.1 The deduction procedure (Payroll, at the pay cut-off)

1

Cut the period. Take every sheet row for the pay period (from the day after the last cut-off to today's cut-off). Copy them to a tab named for the period, e.g. 2026-10; the live tab is never edited.

2

Remove test rows. Delete rows whose CB NUMBER is not a real CB number or a staff username (Test, wimpy.livecheck). They are system checks, not meals.

3

Total per person. Pivot: CB NUMBER → sum of Order Total. One line per person is the proposed deduction.

4

Verify against the ledger. Operations exports the same period from the order ledger (Supabase view wimpy_order_sheet: who, when, items, total, newest first). Per person, ledger total must equal sheet total.

5

Resolve differences before loading. A person in the ledger but not the sheet (or the reverse) means the automation or the ledger write failed for that order — Operations checks the health alerts; the kitchen's completed card decides whether the meal happened.

6

Load. One deduction per person on the payslip, line "Wimpy staff account — <period>". Keep the period tab, the pivot and the ledger export together as the audit record.

7

Queries. A staff query about the amount is answered from My orders (their own screen) and the period tab. Corrections go through Operations, never by editing the live sheet.

3.3 Terms of the staff account

TermRule
WhoEvery active colleague on the Agent List; access ends with the Agent List
PriceThe staff price fixed in the app (about 20% under the Wimpy list price; a third off the toasteds and the Dagwood); the slip total is the amount owed
Cycle and methodDeducted on the payroll run for the period Operations to confirm the cut-off day
LimitThe app allows up to 10 of an item per line and orders only 07:00–10:00 on weekdays; no rand limit in the app Operations to confirm whether a monthly cap applies
LeaversOpen balance settled in the final pay run Operations to confirm
DisputesRaised with the team leader the same morning; settled from My orders, the period tab and the kitchen's card. If the sheet and the ledger disagree, the ledger plus the completed card decide.
Why staff can trust itThe amount comes from the same orders they confirmed on screen and can read in My orders; it is checked against a second, independently written record before it is loaded; test rows are excluded by rule; the audit tab for every period is kept.
