# The Chatterbox — Executive Core
The Chatterbox is a South African compliance-first outsourced sales, contact-centre and revenue-execution business headquartered at **1 Holwood Park, 5 Canegate Road, uMhlanga Ridge, Durban**. It is being transformed from a traditional outbound call centre and point-solution environment into an **AI-native Revenue Execution Platform**.

The platform hierarchy is fixed unless a later signed decision changes it:

- **CIP** runs enterprise governance, systems, actions, decisions, AI, risk, audit and benefits.
- **Clean Sales OS** runs demand, production, QA/compliance and campaign economics.
- **AgentOS** runs the candidate-to-agent lifecycle and Agent Passport.
- **Analytics Upload System** provides controlled manual ingestion where direct integrations are prohibited.

The management rule is: **Nick designs. Management accepts. Managers operate. Data measures. Risk audits. The CEO enforces.**

The economic thesis is: **Scale the revenue floor, not the support floor.**

The governance test is: **If a red metric does not create an owner, action, deadline, evidence and escalation, the system has failed.**
